\section{Estimator}\label{sec:S9}

Source: \code{docs/results/FIX\_EVALUATION.md}.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Headline estimator.} The mean over the 5 repeats of the AUC of the pooled held-out
predictions of each repeat.
\item \textbf{Bootstrap.} Patient-cluster bootstrap, 1,000 draws; each draw resamples patients once and
recomputes the mean-over-repeats statistic of every model on that draw, so differences are paired. $\Delta$AUC
is the point estimate (not the bootstrap mean) with the bootstrap CI.
\item \textbf{Selection inside folds.} Standardisation, imputation and the choice of penalty are all inside the
training fold, the penalty by inner patient-grouped cross validation
\citep{varma2006,cawley2010,demircioglu2021}.
\end{itemize}

\section{SwinUNETR encoder tuned on the task}\label{sec:S22}

\textbf{Plan.} Written into \code{docs/results/FINETUNE\_DEEP.md} and committed before any network saw a
real label; a dated copy (\code{PLAN\_snapshot.md}) was saved at 22:06 on 2026-10-15, and the first real-label job
finished at 22:08. Code: \code{prism/analyses/finetune\_deep.py} (stage \code{finetune}).

\textbf{Splits.} Outer: the shared 5 $\times$ 5 folds. Inside each outer training set, the scans with a known
120 day outcome were split into 4 inner folds grouped by patient. For each inner fold one network was tuned on the
other three, with early stopping on 20\% of their patients, and predicted the inner fold (out-of-fold score) and the
outer test fold. The test score is the mean logit of the 4 networks; the out-of-fold scores, z-scored, were stacked
onto the core model's predictors and the core ridge was refitted. Patients never overlap between fitting, stopping,
inner and outer test sets (unit test). Censored scans were excluded from the training loss and scored as usual.

\textbf{Network.} MONAI SwinUNETR encoder (feature size 48; FLAIR, T1c, T1, T2) from BraTS 2021 or BrainSegFounder
weights. Input: a brain z-scored cube centred on the lesion and cavity, side $\max(128, \text{extent} + 16)$ mm,
resampled to 128$^3$ when larger (37 of 308 scans). Mask-weighted means of stages 2 to 4 within the lesion plus the
global mean of stage 4 (2,112 features), LayerNorm, dropout 0.3 and one linear output. Patch embedding and stages 1--2
frozen; stages 3--4 and the head trained with AdamW (encoder 2e-5, head 1e-3, weight decay 0.01), one warm-up epoch
and cosine decay over 25 epochs, batch 4, bf16, binary cross-entropy; early stopping with patience 5 and at least 3
epochs. Augmentation: flips, translation of $\pm$8 voxels, intensity scale 0.9--1.1 and shift $\pm$0.1. Prediction:
mean over 8 flips. Seeds fixed per job and deterministic kernels; repeated runs are bit identical. No hyperparameter
was tuned.

\textbf{Arms.} (1) BraTS 2021, grade 4 (primary); (2) the same with outcomes permuted within each outer training set
(control; the stacking ridge uses the real outcomes); (3) BraTS 2021, all gliomas; (4) BrainSegFounder, grade 4; (5)
optional, a siamese network that also sees the previous scan, cropped at the same atlas box. Each arm has 100
fine-tunes.

\textbf{Rule.} Fine-tuning helps if, in grade 4, the core model plus the arm 1 score gains at least 0.03 with a paired
95\% CI excluding 0. Validity: the permuted arm's image-only AUC near 0.5 with a CI covering 0.5, and its stack gain
$\leq$ 0. Other arms are secondary, without multiplicity correction.

\section{Report content against the BT-RADS follow-up template}\label{sec:S24}

Source: the BT-RADS follow-up reporting template (sample v1.03, btrads.com) and category guide (version 1.01)
\citep{weinberg2018}, and the report fields in \code{prism/report/facts\_v3.py} and \code{prism/report/sections\_v3.py}.
No patient record was used for this comparison.

\textbf{Why this template.} No public cohort releases clinician reports for glioma follow-up MRI after treatment,
including the three used here, so the report could not be compared with the reports written for the same scans. We
compared its content instead with the structured follow-up template of the Brain Tumor Reporting and Data System
(BT-RADS) \citep{weinberg2018}. In surveys of referring clinicians at the centre that introduced it, reports
written with the template were judged more consistent and less ambiguous than free prose \citep{gore2019}, and they
recorded more treatment history and used fewer hedging words \citep{zhang2020}. In a 2018 survey of 220 European
centres, 60\% issued qualitative follow-up reports and 23\% used a template \citep{thust2018}.

\section{Analyses rerun with the restored data}\label{sec:S25}

% Sources: docs/results/REVISION_DATA_PLAN.md (before the run); REVISION_DATA.md; NUMBERS.md 16.
These analyses answer three limits of the planned system, a conformal set taken from a refitted model, a reliability
card that counts the scan it describes and the lack of a template baseline, and they regenerate the reports with the
model names used in this paper. The rules were written before the run. They are not part of the earlier
registered plans and carry a dagger in the main text. The UCSF-ALPTDG images were not available for this rerun, so
every result is for MU-Glioma-Post and LUMIERE. Before any new analysis, every registered score, AUC, coverage and
error rate of these two cohorts was rebuilt from the raw data and matched the published values.

\paragraph{Conformal sets from the displayed score.} The planned set refits the combined model on 70\% of the training

\subsection{AUC split by context}\label{sec:S26.1}

from different contexts, and computed the AUC within each set. With $w$ the share of pairs from the

radiotherapy, with 1 progression and 27 other scans flagged and 72 and 78 not flagged. The MU values of $A_W$

\subsection{Bound verifier}\label{sec:S26.3}

We added two rules to the frozen checker, which itself was not edited. The first binds each number
that sits next to a named quantity (enhancing tumour, FLAIR hyperintensity, necrosis, cavity, or one of the three
model scores) to a cited field for that quantity. The second binds each direction word (larger, increased, smaller,
decreased and similar) to every cited field that carries a direction. The rules of version 1.0 were fixed before any
report was checked. They bound a number only to cited fields whose key names the quantity, and they
rejected every real report (276 of 276 from the language model and 276 of 276 from the template) and 24 of the 52
correct synthetic reports, because the statement fields name the quantity in their text and not in their key. This
pre-specified rule failed, and we report it as such. Version 1.1 also binds numbers and direction
words inside a cited text field to the quantity named in their clause. It was developed on the synthetic records
(52 of 52 correct reports passed) and on the development reports (47 of 48 from the language model and 48 of 48 from
the template), frozen, and then applied once to the evaluation reports.

Every evaluation report passed the frozen checker. Version 1.1 rejected 1 of the 114 MU reports of the language model
(by the direction rule), none of its 162 LUMIERE reports and none of the 276 template reports. These rejections were
not adjudicated, because the reports are not read in a hosted context. Table S15 gives the share of planted errors
rejected. The plan counts the verifier as closing a blind spot when it rejects at least 95\% of the synthetic mutants
