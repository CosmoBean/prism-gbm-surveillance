# System landscape: does anything like PRISM already exist?

As of 2026-10-25. Focused web search (not a systematic review), 2020–2026, peer-reviewed preferred, arXiv/medRxiv included where relevant. A few pre-2020 papers are included only where they are the canonical precedent for a component.

This file complements `RELATED_WORK.md` (task-level literature: PsP classifiers, forward progression, datasets, RANO, baselines, validation methods) and `COMPARABLE_RESULTS.md` (numeric comparators). Papers already there are not repeated; they are cross-referenced as "(in RELATED_WORK)" or "(in COMPARABLE_RESULTS)".

**The system being positioned.** Post-treatment glioma surveillance MRI → deep segmentation → engineered measurements (volumes, change from previous scan and from nadir); a separate clinical + treatment-timeline pathway; both shown side by side (no learned fusion layer) with a combined score; a per-scan reliability statement looked up from measured performance in that clinical situation (phase of care × enhancement state); conformal prediction sets; flags (pathway disagreement, out of training range); an LLM-written report grounded only in the per-scan record and checked by a deterministic faithfulness checker; locked tests at two external centres.

**Verification key.** ✓ = citation opened and checked this pass (publisher page, PubMed/Europe PMC record, Crossref record, arXiv API, or FDA database). ✓(meta) = bibliographic record verified, but content summarised from the abstract/search snippet because the full page was blocked (HTTP 403). ✗ = could not verify; listed only in the last section and not to be cited as is.

These are re-implementations on a new centre, so they show typical drops to near chance.

**Finding.** Locked tests at **two** independent external centres are uncommon for post-treatment glioma progression/PsP models. Most studies use one external set, a temporal split, or none. Typical drops are about 0.05–0.15 AUC, and occasionally to chance. We did not run a count; this rests on Booth 2022, Shi 2025 and Kelly 2022.

## Conclusions

### (a) Components that are established

- **Segmentation → volumes → change between scans** in glioma follow-up is mature. It exists in academic tools (AutoRANO, Rudie 2022, Kickingereder 2019, Vollmuth 2023, RANO2.0-assist) and in FDA-cleared products (Neosoma NS-HGlio, Cortechs OnQ Neuro/NeuroQuant). None predicts future progression, and none gives a per-scan reliability statement.
- **Combining clinical/treatment-timeline variables with volumetrics** exists by rule in RANO/BT-RADS and has recently been automated with LLM extraction (Jabal 2026). Learned fusion exists for PsP (Gomaa 2025).
- **Conformal prediction** is established in medical imaging, including subgroup-conditional coverage (Lu 2022), and has reached glioma *segmentation* (CONSeg 2025). Selective prediction and deferral are established in imaging (CoDoC 2023; Kompa 2021).
- **Subgroup-disaggregated performance and point-of-care disclosure** are established as model-level documentation (model cards, Model Facts labels, the FDA/HC/MHRA 2024 principles). Case-level reliability from similar cases has existed since Habas 2007.
- **Shortcut learning and confounding by healthcare-process timing** are well documented (Geirhos, DeGrave, Badgeley, Agniel).
- **"LLM writes, deterministic code verifies"** exists outside imaging and in chest X-ray logic checking (Zeba 2026, PROVE 2026, Singh 2026).
- **External drops** of about 6 % (radiology overall) and about 0.05–0.15 AUC (PsP) are documented. Two-centre locked tests are uncommon.

### (b) What appears new, and how strongly we can say it

Suggested wording: *"We found no published system for post-treatment glioma surveillance that pairs an imaging pathway with a separate clinical and treatment-timeline pathway and gives, for each scan, a reliability statement looked up from measured external performance in that scan's clinical situation (phase of care × enhancement), together with conformal prediction sets, disagreement and out-of-range flags, and a language-model report restricted to the per-scan record and checked by a deterministic program, evaluated locked at two external centres."*

Two narrower claims can be stated with reasonable confidence:

1. We found no glioma progression/PsP model that reports performance stratified by phase of care, or that treats phase of care as a shortcut. This matches the earlier search in COMPARABLE_RESULTS.
2. We found no application of conformal prediction sets or abstention to a post-treatment progression call. Conformal prediction in glioma has so far been applied to segmentation and molecular markers.

**Strength:** moderate. This was a focused web search, not a systematic review. Agentic and LLM preprints in this area appear monthly; Jabal 2026 and Erdur 2026 both appeared this year. Commercial products may have undocumented features: we saw FDA summaries, not product interfaces. Avoid "first" or "novel system" wording. Credit Jabal 2026 explicitly as the nearest system: it combines clinical variables, volumetrics and an LLM, but classifies the current scan at a single centre and reports no uncertainty. Each component is borrowed. The contribution is the combination and the per-scan reliability lookup, tested on two locked external centres.

### (c) The 5–8 most important references to cite

1. **Shi et al. 2025, *Neuro-Oncology*** (1.7): a systematic review that names the gaps PRISM fills (clinical integration, warnings, external tests).
2. **Jabal et al. 2026, arXiv** (1.13): the closest system; cite it and draw the contrast.
3. **Vollmuth et al. 2023, *Neuro-Oncology*** (1.2): AI volumetric decision support changes response assessment.
