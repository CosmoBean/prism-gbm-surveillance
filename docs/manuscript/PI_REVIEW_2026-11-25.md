## Results to report

- ✅ **"We can't really show a marginal increase."**
  - The paper no longer argues for a gain of one model over another. The planned test is still reported as planned,
    with no clear gain over the time-and-enhancement model (Table 2). The text now reports what each score rests on.
- ✅ **"Do we have a mind-blowing one split result (AUC-ROC)?"**
  - PRISM's combined model was refitted and scored on 100 random held-out sets of 18 of the 92 grade 4 patients,
    the design of the earlier study on this cohort. The best set reached an AUC of 1.00, the average was 0.84, and 95%
    of the sets fell between 0.68 and 0.96 (exploratory; abstract, Sections 2.3 and 3.1).
  - With the same design, the earlier radiomics re-implementation ranged from 0.32 to 0.88 (mean 0.63).
- ✅ **"How are the models performing on LUMIERE and UCSF, and what has been cited by the literature?"**
  - Without retraining, the combined model reached 0.79 at LUMIERE and 0.81 at UCSF-ALPTDG with PRISM's segmenter, and
    0.77 and 0.86 with the supplied masks (abstract, Section 3.1).
  - A literature search for models tested on these cohorts found no published result directly comparable to ours,
    so the paper makes no priority claim.
