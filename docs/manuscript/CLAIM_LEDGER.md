# Claim ledger

One row per claim the paper makes (`paper/main.tex`, figure titles in `scripts/generate_system_first_figures.py` and
`scripts/fig1_prism.py`), traced to its evidence. Built 2026-11-02 against the paper at commit 3551d8a. Update a row
whenever the paper's wording or a number changes; a claim with no row should not be in the paper.

**Status key.** PLANNED-MET / PLANNED-NOT-MET: a rule fixed in advance was / was not met. EXPLORATORY: analysis not
fixed in advance (daggered in the paper). DESIGN: a property of the system, not a result. PRIOR-WORK: positioning
against the literature (limits in `docs/literature/PRIOR_ART_2026-11-01.md`). LIMITATION. INTERPRETATION: an inference
from results. UNVERIFIED: no source found. MISMATCH: the paper and its source disagree.

Sources are documents in `docs/`; `data/` and `outputs/` were wiped on 2026-11-02, so the numbers trace to
`docs/manuscript/NUMBERS.md` and `docs/results/`, not to regenerated files. Aggregate numbers only.
