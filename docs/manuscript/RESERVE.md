# The reserve: questions we leave to the reviewers

Results that are true, checked and ready, but deliberately not in the main text (the 80 / 20 rule,
[STYLE.md](STYLE.md) section 4). Each row gives the likely reviewer question, the short answer, and where the full
answer lives. When a reviewer asks, the response quotes the answer and points to the supplement section.

Rule: an item may sit here only if it leaves the paper's conclusion unchanged. Anything that would change what a
reader concludes belongs in the main text.

| Likely question | Short answer | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Is the phase effect an artefact of censoring? | No (censoring applies to the secondary 120-day task; detection negatives need 120 days of follow-up). Censoring sits almost entirely before RT (38 of 39 censored scans); phase-specific weights raised the phase gain (+0.080 → +0.124) and excluding patients with undocumented RT left +0.057. In the intended use, 1 scan is censored. | Supplementary S20, S23; `docs/results/INTENDED_USE.md` |
| Would deaths counted as non-events change anything? | 120-day task: absolute AUCs move (corrected core 0.706); no imaging contrast moves by more than 0.011. | S23 |
| Did you try enough imaging? | Radiomics over 70 configurations, frozen encoders over 32, fine-tuned SwinUNETR / BrainSegFounder (forward: 5 arms; detection: 9 arms incl. siamese and subtraction inputs) with shuffled-label controls, resegmentation with two networks, an imaging-defined label, and ADC in CFB-GBM. None beat the timeline pathway in detection on MU (Table 2, first two rows). | Table 3 / S13, S14, S18, S22; `docs/results/DETECTION_DEEP.md` |
| Why did the fine-tuned networks fail at detection? | Image-only AUC 0.45–0.51, indistinguishable from the permuted-label control (0.48); about 87 training scans per network, early stopping at epochs 0–3. With few positives, the networks never learned what the volume measurements encode. | `docs/results/DETECTION_DEEP.md` |
| Why is a pooled AUC below 0.5 for some deep arms? | Within each fold the AUC is about 0.50; pooling across folds lowers it because training and test prevalence are anti-correlated (r = −0.99). | `DETECTION_DEEP.md`, exploratory diagnostics |
| Are the volumes trustworthy? | Our volumes reproduce the dataset's own table (r ≥ 0.996); resegmentation agrees (median ET Dice 0.80). Volume series are noisy: median 3.2-fold ET change between scans ≤ 14 days apart. | S14; `docs/results/DIAGNOSTICS_IMAGING.md` |
| Is ridge the right learner? | Gradient boosting did not beat ridge (core 0.687 vs 0.698). | Table 2 / S12 |
| How well calibrated is it? | 120-day model, corrected core: observed / expected 1.04, slope 0.85; intended use O/E 1.01, slope 0.53 (spread too wide because part of the range encodes pre- vs post-RT); LUMIERE O/E 0.99, slope 0.97. | S12, S23 |
| Which features does the model rely on? | SHAP gives imaging blocks about half of the credit in the full models without any gain in AUC; attribution is not evidence of value. | Fig. (ablation C) / S12 |
| Does the external test hold? | LUMIERE, locked: detection 0.77 with imaging adding +0.08 (−0.01 to +0.16) over the reduced timeline; the secondary 120-day forecast reached 0.635 (corrected core). LUMIERE has no treatment dates and rating-defined negatives. | S13; `docs/results/DETECTION_TASK.md` |
| What exactly is in the imaging-only detection model? | Volumes, shape and relative intensity at the scan; volume ratios to the previous scan; ratio to the smallest volume over the whole history. The version with days since the previous scan, a has-previous flag and an RT-anchored nadir window scored 0.81; those three inputs encode the schedule. | `DETECTION_TASK.md`, exploratory section; `scripts/exploratory_pure_imaging_detection.py` |
| Could the report generator be gamed, or is it model-specific? | The checker is deterministic; a smaller model (Qwen3.5-9B) passed 243 / 249; reports followed the changed field in all 30 perturbed records; the original ungrounded prompt gave advice in 99% of reports. | S19, S21 |
| Why not a forward triage model? | Volume-trajectory features add −0.008 in the intended use; no rule-out threshold reaches an NPV lower bound of 0.85 (best 0.66). A triage model needs multicentre data, expert-read labels and perfusion. | `docs/results/TRIAGE_FEASIBILITY.md` |
| How does this relate to Christodoulou 2026 (0.80 on MU-Glioma-Post)? | Different label (progression status of each scan, i.e. detection) and one held-out set; we did not re-evaluate it. Our detection timing model alone reaches 0.82 on this dataset. | Discussion (one sentence); `docs/literature/COMPARABLE_RESULTS.md` |
| The UCSF plan said "all MU detection scans"; why were the locked models fitted on grade 4 rows? | A deviation, decided before UCSF labels were read; the all-glioma model gives 0.768 against 0.773 at LUMIERE and the same picture at UCSF. One clause in Methods 2.3 and the Table 2 note. | `docs/results/UCSF_RESULTS.md` Part A; supplement deviations |
| Why did the change inputs transfer worse to UCSF than single-scan measurements? | UCSF change between its two scans spans a range three to six times narrower than at MU; single-scan 0.80 vs change 0.73 (difference −0.07, −0.13 to −0.01). | `UCSF_RESULTS.md` contrasts |
| Is a one-split evaluation trustworthy at UCSF? | No single split is: UCSF's own train/test split gave a UCSF-trained timeline 0.45 against a median of 0.72 over 100 random splits (1st percentile); we used repeated CV. | Supp. S8; `UCSF_RESULTS.md` Part B |
| Why not use PRISM's own segmentation for the measurements? | Pre-specified resegmentation of MU with a BraTS 2021 SwinUNETR agreed with the MU masks (median ET Dice 0.80) and did not change detection (−0.016, −0.057 to +0.025), so the cohort masks were kept. | S14; `docs/results/RESEGMENTATION.md` |
| Is there a flag that works at all three centres? | Exploratory only: "disagree and set holds both outcomes" flags 12–27%; flagged error 0.52 / 0.54 / 0.38 vs 0.22 / 0.22 / 0.25 (UCSF difference CI includes 0); chosen after the planned results; untested on new data. One labelled sentence in 3.4. | `SYSTEM_RESULTS.md` exploratory (3d20c41) |
